## CHARMM36 / CGenFF force field

`src/memback/data/forcefields/charmm36-feb2026_cgenff-5.0.ff/`

GROMACS port of the CHARMM36 additive force field with CGenFF 5.0, generated
with `charmm2gmx` v1.0.4 (February 2026 release).

- Port by András Wacha and Justin A. Lemkul, based on the original port by
  E. Prabhu Raman, Justin A. Lemkul, Robert Best and Alexander D. MacKerell, Jr.
- Converter: https://gitlab.com/awacha/charmm2gmx
- Upstream releases: https://github.com/Lemkul-Lab/toppar_c36_releases
- Distribution point: MacKerell lab, http://mackerell.umaryland.edu/charmm_ff.shtml

Cite:

> A. F. Wacha and J. A. Lemkul. charmm2gmx: An Automated Method to Port the
> CHARMM Additive Force Field to GROMACS. *J. Chem. Inf. Model.* **2023**,
> 63(14), 4246–4252. doi:10.1021/acs.jcim.3c00860

Plus the underlying parameter papers listed in `forcefield.doc` inside that
directory — in particular Klauda et al. 2010 (doi:10.1021/jp101759q) for lipids
and Huang et al. 2016 (doi:10.1038/nmeth.4067) for CHARMM36m.

## CHARMM lipid topologies

`src/memback/data/charmm_lipid_itps/` — 440 per-lipid `.itp` files.

Generated by the **CHARMM-GUI Force Field Converter**. The file headers give
correspondence addresses at Lehigh University (jul316@lehigh.edu,
wonpil@lehigh.edu).

> ⚠️ CHARMM-GUI output is subject to the CHARMM-GUI terms of use, which place
> conditions on redistribution. Before publishing this repository, check
> https://charmm-gui.org and contact the CHARMM-GUI team if redistribution is
> not clearly permitted. If it is not, the practical alternative is to ship a
> download script rather than the files themselves.

Cite:

> S. Jo, T. Kim, V. G. Iyer, W. Im. CHARMM-GUI: A Web-based Graphical User
> Interface for CHARMM. *J. Comput. Chem.* **2008**, 29, 1859–1865.

## Martini 3 lipid topologies

`src/memback/data/m3_itps/`

Martini 3 lipidome parameters, last updated December 2024 by Kasper B. Pedersen.

Cite:

> K. B. Pedersen et al. The Martini 3 Lipidome: Expanded and Refined Parameters
> Improve Lipid Phase Behavior. *ACS Cent. Sci.* **2025**.
> doi:10.1021/acscentsci.5c00755

> P. C. T. Souza et al. Martini 3: A General Purpose Force Field for
> Coarse-Grained Molecular Dynamics. *Nat. Methods* **2021**, 18, 382–388.
> doi:10.1038/s41592-021-01098-3

## Mapping and bond files

`src/memback/data/maps/all_maps.map`, `all_maps.bnd`

AA↔CG mappings and CG bonded definitions in PyCGTOOL format, derived from the
Martini 3 lipid definitions above.

## Hydrogen database

`src/memback/data/hdb/lipid.hdb`

Derived from the CHARMM36 residue topology, in the GROMACS `.hdb` format.

## Model checkpoint

`src/memback/model/memback_0.1.1_state_dict.pt`

Trained as part of this project and covered by the repository license, though
note that it was trained on data derived from the force fields above.

## Runtime dependencies

Installed by pip rather than bundled; listed for licence-compatibility
reference.

| Package | License |
| --- | --- |
| PyTorch | BSD-3-Clause |
| PyTorch Geometric | MIT |
| MDAnalysis (≥ 2.8) | LGPL-3.0-or-later (package); contributions LGPL-2.1-or-later |
| NumPy | BSD-3-Clause |
| lmdb (py-lmdb) | OpenLDAP Public License |
| tqdm | MPL-2.0 / MIT |

All are compatible with distributing MemBack under GPL-3.0-or-later. MDAnalysis
being LGPL rather than GPL matters here: it means MemBack is free to choose its
own license, and a GPL choice is one of the options that remains open.

GROMACS is invoked as a separate program by the generated `run_min.sh` and is
not linked into MemBack, so its LGPL-2.1 terms do not propagate.
